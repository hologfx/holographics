# Contributing to Holographics

Thanks for your interest in contributing! Issues and pull requests are welcome.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Before we can merge your first pull request, you must sign our
[Contributor License Agreement](.github/CLA.md). A bot will comment on your
pull request with instructions — you sign once by replying with a short
comment, and it's remembered for all future PRs. You keep ownership of your
contribution; the CLA grants
Stream My Event B.V. the rights needed to keep licensing the project
(including under the AGPL and commercial licenses).

## Guidelines

- Open an issue first for anything larger than a bug fix, so we can discuss
  the approach before you invest time.
- Run `npm test` before submitting.
- Keep pull requests focused — one change per PR.
